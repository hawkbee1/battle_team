# HQ and Storage

Status: **ready**. Decisions come from the Q&A with the game designer; changes go through `specs/95-open-questions.md`. Each rule has a stable ID (`HQ-NN`).

## 1. Role of the HQ
- **HQ-01** Each team has one HQ, placed at its starting point at the beginning of a battle.
- **HQ-02** Commanders **resurrect in storage** after death, with their section (decision 125); they deploy from their storage place like any unit.
- **HQ-03** The HQ is a **storage place** for sections that are not in a guild (see section 5).
- **HQ-04** Hitting and destroying an enemy HQ **earns money**.

## 2. HQ manager
- **HQ-05** The player of the **highest-ranking commander** of the team manages the HQ, and can **delegate** this role to another player.
- **HQ-06** **Highest-ranking** = the player with the **most medal points**. (Depends on the medal system, still to be defined.)
- **HQ-07** The manager is **fixed at battle start**. It changes only by delegation during the battle.
- **HQ-08** **No fallback** if the manager is offline: he must delegate before leaving, otherwise nobody can move the HQ or post team orders until he returns.
- **HQ-09** Powers of the HQ manager:
  - post **team-wide orders and map markers**, seen by the whole team;
  - **move the HQ**.

## 3. Moving the HQ
- **HQ-10** The manager decides to move the HQ; units of the team **push it, like a relic**: minimum number of pushers, AP cost for each pusher, fixed slow speed, slope changes the effort.
- **HQ-11** Same obstacles as relics: no flying, no water except reinforced bridges, no forests, towns or walls.
- **HQ-12** A pushed HQ **does not crush** units: it **pushes them sideways** out of its path.
- **HQ-13** Storage and resurrection keep working while it moves.

- **HQ-14** **Any unit of the team** can push the HQ once the manager has ordered the move. (Decision 109)
- **HQ-15** Pushing follows the relic rules: at least 10 pushers, AP per metre, slope as for movement (decision 98).

## 4. Destruction and elimination
- **HQ-16** The HQ has **hit points**. Enemy units damage it until 0, then it is destroyed.
- **HQ-17** **No HQ repair, no HQ weapons, no HQ-specific defenses**: the HQ is defended by units only. Engineers can still build **ordinary walls** near it, like anywhere else.
- **HQ-18** A team whose HQ is destroyed is **eliminated** from the battle.
- **HQ-19** Its **stored units are not affected**: they stay in storage for the next battle of the war, if any.
- **HQ-20** Its **units still on the field are automatically stored** for the next battle.
- **HQ-21** Eliminated players can **join another running battle** with another of their commanders, if they have one (one commander per battle rule still applies).

- **HQ-22** HQ hit points: **1000** (battle setting, `data/battle.yaml`), armor type `hq`. (Decisions 109, 114)
- **HQ-23** An HQ on a reinforced bridge that is destroyed is **lost**: its team is eliminated (decisions 99, 116).

## 5. Storage
### 5.0 Lore: storage is the homeland
- **HQ-24** Stored units are not physically inside the HQ or the encampment: they are back in their **homeland**.
- **HQ-25** HQs and guild encampments contain a **teleportation portal** to the homeland. Only **authorized units** can use a portal (the storage rules below define who is authorized where).
- **HQ-26** Consequence: destroying an HQ or an encampment **never affects stored units**; it only closes that portal.

### 5.1 Where a section stores its units
- **HQ-27** A section **in a guild** stores its units in its **guild encampment**.
- **HQ-28** A section **not in any guild** stores its units in the **HQ**.
- **HQ-29** Stored units cannot be attacked and are not visible.
- **HQ-30** Storage capacity is **limited per section** and **grows with the commander level** (from section XP, decision 79), like the section size cap.

### 5.2 Storing
- **HQ-31** **Immediate**: a unit **within 10 m** of its storage place (guild encampment, or HQ for sections without guild) is stored at once. (Decision 110)
- **HQ-32** **Recall from anywhere**: a unit elsewhere on the map can be recalled after **6 hours**. Nothing stops a recall once started (attacks or movement do not interrupt it); the unit can still die during the delay. It goes to its section's storage place (encampment or HQ, as in 5.1).

Design note: since a recall cannot be interrupted, the **length of the delay** is the only thing preventing units from escaping fights. It must be long enough that a recall started in combat rarely saves a unit.

### 5.3 Deploying
- **HQ-33** Stored units are deployed **instantly next to their storage place**. Deploying **costs the unit 10 AP**; it is then fully operational. (Decision 110)
- **HQ-34** **Stored units regenerate AP** up to their maximum.
- **HQ-35** **Full storage lock** (decision 120): once **every alive unit** of a section is stored, the section **cannot deploy again for 24 hours** (battle setting). This is how a player takes a break: he never leaves the battle. A storage forced by the **commander's death** (decision 80) does **not** start the lock (decision 125).
- **HQ-36** **Deployment is blocked while an enemy unit is within 20 m** of the storage place (the guild encampment, or the HQ for sections without guild).
- **HQ-37** Prisoners are kept in a separate, unlimited prisoner storage (decision 94).

### 5.4 Guild encampment rules
- **HQ-38** A guild **without an encampment** stores its sections' units in the **HQ**.
- **HQ-39** If the encampment is **blocked** by an enemy, its sections **cannot deploy from the HQ instead**: they must clear the enemy first.
- **HQ-40** An encampment is destroyed **like any other unit** (it has hit points and dies at 0).
- **HQ-41** Units stored by the guild are **not affected** by the destruction (they are in the homeland). Until a new encampment exists, the guild's sections use the HQ portal ("no encampment ⇒ HQ").
- **HQ-42** The guild can **buy a new encampment at any time**.

- **HQ-43** Guild encampment: **500 w€**, **300 HP**, armor type `structure` (decisions 111, 114). It cannot reach floating islands (decision 88).
- **HQ-44** Storage capacity per commander level: `data/units.yaml` (`progression.commander_levels`).

## 6. Link with victory
- **HQ-45** A battle ends when only one HQ remains (or by the relic rule). With several teams, each HQ destruction eliminates one team.
