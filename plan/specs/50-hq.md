# HQ and Storage (working draft)

Status: draft. Decisions come from the Q&A with the game designer.

## 1. Role of the HQ
- Each team has one HQ, placed at its starting point at the beginning of a battle.
- Commanders **resurrect at the HQ** after death.
- The HQ is a **storage place** for sections that are not in a guild (see section 5).
- Hitting and destroying an enemy HQ **earns money**.

## 2. HQ manager
- The player of the **highest-ranking commander** of the team manages the HQ, and can **delegate** this role to another player.
- **Highest-ranking** = the player with the **most medal points**. (Depends on the medal system, still to be defined.)
- The manager is **fixed at battle start**. It changes only by delegation during the battle.
- **No fallback** if the manager is offline: he must delegate before leaving, otherwise nobody can move the HQ or post team orders until he returns.
- Powers of the HQ manager:
  - post **team-wide orders and map markers**, seen by the whole team;
  - **move the HQ**.

## 3. Moving the HQ
- The manager decides to move the HQ; units of the team **push it, like a relic**: minimum number of pushers, AP cost for each pusher, fixed slow speed, slope changes the effort.
- Same obstacles as relics: no flying, no water except reinforced bridges, no forests, towns or walls.
- A pushed HQ **does not crush** units: it **pushes them sideways** out of its path.
- Storage and resurrection keep working while it moves.

Open:
- Can any team unit push the HQ, or only units chosen by the manager?

## 4. Destruction and elimination
- The HQ has **hit points**. Enemy units damage it until 0, then it is destroyed.
- **No HQ repair, no HQ weapons, no HQ-specific defenses**: the HQ is defended by units only. Engineers can still build **ordinary walls** near it, like anywhere else.
- A team whose HQ is destroyed is **eliminated** from the battle.
- Its **stored units are not affected**: they stay in storage for the next battle of the war, if any.
- Its **units still on the field are automatically stored** for the next battle.
- Eliminated players can **join another running battle** with another of their commanders, if they have one (one commander per battle rule still applies).

- HQ hit points and armor.

## 5. Storage
### 5.0 Lore: storage is the homeland
- Stored units are not physically inside the HQ or the encampment: they are back in their **homeland**.
- HQs and guild encampments contain a **teleportation portal** to the homeland. Only **authorized units** can use a portal (the storage rules below define who is authorized where).
- Consequence: destroying an HQ or an encampment **never affects stored units**; it only closes that portal.

### 5.1 Where a section stores its units
- A section **in a guild** stores its units in its **guild encampment**.
- A section **not in any guild** stores its units in the **HQ**.
- Stored units cannot be attacked and are not visible.
- Storage capacity is **limited per section** and **grows with the commander level** (from section XP, decision 79), like the section size cap.

### 5.2 Storing
- **Immediate**: a unit **next to** its storage place (guild encampment, or HQ for sections without guild) is stored at once.
- **Recall from anywhere**: a unit elsewhere on the map can be recalled after a **long delay**. Nothing stops a recall once started (attacks or movement do not interrupt it); the unit can still die during the delay. It goes to its section's storage place (encampment or HQ, as in 5.1).

Design note: since a recall cannot be interrupted, the **length of the delay** is the only thing preventing units from escaping fights. It must be long enough that a recall started in combat rarely saves a unit.

### 5.3 Deploying
- Stored units are deployed **instantly next to their storage place**.
- **Deployment is blocked while an enemy unit is next to** the storage place (the guild encampment, or the HQ for sections without guild).

### 5.4 Guild encampment rules
- A guild **without an encampment** stores its sections' units in the **HQ**.
- If the encampment is **blocked** by an enemy, its sections **cannot deploy from the HQ instead**: they must clear the enemy first.
- An encampment is destroyed **like any other unit** (it has hit points and dies at 0).
- Units stored by the guild are **not affected** by the destruction (they are in the homeland). Until a new encampment exists, the guild's sections use the HQ portal ("no encampment ⇒ HQ").
- The guild can **buy a new encampment at any time**.

Open:
- Recall delay value; does a recall cost AP or prevent attacking?
- Formula of storage capacity vs commander XP (data file).
- Radius of "next to" for storing and for blocking deployment.
- Encampment price and hit points.

## 6. Link with victory
- A battle ends when only one HQ remains (or by the relic rule). With several teams, each HQ destruction eliminates one team.
